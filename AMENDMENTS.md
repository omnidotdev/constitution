# Amendment Process

This constitution is a living document. It must be able to evolve as the ecosystem and its community grow. However, changes to foundational commitments must be deliberate, transparent, and difficult enough to prevent erosion by convenience.

## Amendment Categories

### Constitutional Amendments

Changes to PRINCIPLES.md or RIGHTS.md require the highest threshold of review. These documents represent the foundation of trust between Omni and its community.

**Process:**
1. **Proposal** — submit as a pull request with a detailed rationale explaining why the change is necessary and what problem it solves
2. **Public comment period** — minimum 30 days for community discussion
3. **Review** — governance stewards evaluate the proposal, community feedback, and potential consequences
4. **Ratification** — requires supermajority approval from governance stewards

No constitutional amendment may remove or weaken a right defined in RIGHTS.md without the explicit, documented consent of the community it affects.

### Operational Amendments

Changes to AMENDMENTS.md itself, or additions of new governance documents, follow the same process as constitutional amendments. The rules for changing the rules must be as protected as the rules themselves.

### Preamble Revisions

The preamble may be revised to better articulate Omni's mission as it evolves, but its core intent — commitment to open, user-sovereign, community-governed technology — may not be contradicted.

## Current Governance Structure

### Founding Phase (current)

During the founding phase, governance authority rests with the project founder. This is a practical necessity, not a permanent state. The founder is bound by this constitution and may not act in contradiction to its principles or rights.

**Transition commitment:** As the Omni community grows, governance will progressively decentralize. Specific triggers and mechanisms for this transition will be defined and ratified as amendments to this document.

### Future Phases

The following governance structures are under consideration for future adoption. Each will be formally proposed and ratified through the amendment process when the community reaches sufficient maturity:

- **Governance council** — elected or rotating seats representing contributors, users, and ecosystem partners
- **RFC process** — structured proposal and review system for technical and policy decisions
- **Working groups** — domain-specific bodies with delegated authority over their area of expertise

## Record Keeping

All amendments, proposals (accepted and rejected), and governance decisions are recorded in this repository's git history. The commit log serves as the canonical record of how and why this constitution evolved.

## Interpretation

When the text of this constitution is ambiguous, it must be interpreted in favor of user sovereignty, community rights, and openness. In all cases, the spirit of the preamble governs.
