# Preamble

We build Omni because we believe the tools people use every day should belong to the people who use them.

The modern digital landscape is fractured — walled gardens extract value from users, proprietary platforms hold data hostage, and the software that powers daily life is controlled by entities whose incentives diverge from the people they serve. We reject this trajectory.

Omni exists to build an ecosystem of interconnected, open, human-centered tools that empower individuals and communities to own their digital lives. Not as a product to be consumed, but as infrastructure to be shared.

We hold these commitments not because they are easy or profitable in the short term, but because they are right. This constitution binds us — the creators, maintainers, and stewards of Omni — to a set of principles and rights that no business pressure, competitive landscape, or growth imperative can override.

We write this document now, while the choices are still ours alone, so that as Omni grows, these values are not ours to abandon. They belong to everyone who builds with us, builds on us, and trusts us with their work.

This is our foundation. Everything we build stands on it.
